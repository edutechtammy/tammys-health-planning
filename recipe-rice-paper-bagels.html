🔧 Technique: 4-layer stack → roll → bake

Rice Paper Bagels

Four soaked rice paper sheets stacked and rolled into a ring — baked at 400°F until the outside blisters and crisps while the compressed inner layers stay chewy. The chew is entirely a function of the layering: one sheet makes a chip, four sheets make a bagel. No flour, no yeast, no gluten. Topped with garlic, onion flakes, and herbs (sesame and poppy omitted for oxalate). Served with labneh, smoked salmon, cucumber, capers, and fresh dill — an APOE4-targeted breakfast built around omega-3, probiotic fat, and essentially zero starch once the topping is on.

⏱ 15 min prep + 20 min bake 🫓 Makes 4 bagels 🌡 400°F oven 🌾 GF — naturally gluten-free

🔧 The technique — why 4 layers and why it works
📐 Layer count is everything — this is not intuitive

Rice paper is almost entirely starch. When a single sheet dries out in the oven it has nothing to bond to — it crisps and puffs like a cracker. When you stack 4 sheets, the layers bond to each other under heat and compression. The inner layers cannot dry out at the same rate as the outer layers, so they stay pliable and chewy while the outside blisters. This is the same logic as phyllo pastry — the layering is the technique.

Layer 4 (outermost) — direct heat → crispy, blistered
Layer 3 — semi-crisp, slight chew
Layer 2 — chewy, dense
Layer 1 (innermost, rolled tightly) — chewy core
→ Result: crispy outside, chewy inside — bagel-like texture without flour, yeast, or gluten

⏱ Soaking time is the other variable

~15 seconds in warm water — just pliable, not fully soft. If you over-soak (30+ seconds), the sheet tears during rolling and won't hold its shape. If you under-soak (5 seconds), the layers don't bond. Aim for the sheet to be flexible but still have slight resistance when lifted. It will continue softening from residual moisture as you layer.

✅ The self-adhesive advantage

Wet rice paper is tacky — it sticks to itself and to the baking parchment. No egg wash, no water seal, no glue needed. Press the seam of the ring together firmly for 10 seconds and it will hold through the full bake. This is also why you layer directly — you don't need anything between sheets.

⚠️ Topping note — sesame seeds and poppy seeds are high oxalate

Traditional everything bagel seasoning contains both sesame seeds (~90mg oxalate per tablespoon) and poppy seeds (~25mg per tablespoon) — high enough to matter for kidney stone prevention. This recipe uses garlic flakes + onion flakes + dried rosemary/thyme instead. Same aromatic flavour profile, essentially zero oxalate. If you don't have kidney stone concerns, sesame seeds in small amounts are fine — note them as a topping-to-measure rather than to pour.

🧾 Ingredients & Method
🧾 Ingredients — 4 bagels
  • Bagel shells (16 sheets total, 4 per bagel)
  • 16 sheetsRice paper (22cm round)~zero ox
  • 1 tspGarlic flakes~zero ox
  • 1 tspDried onion flakes~zero ox
  • 1 tspDried rosemary or thyme~zero ox
  • lightOlive oil spray or EVOO brush~zero ox
  • Classic serving (APOE4-targeted)
  • 4 tbspLabneh (or strained Greek yogurt)~zero ox
  • 80gSmoked salmon (wild-caught preferred)~zero ox
  • ½Cucumber, thinly sliced~2mg ox
  • 2 tspCapers, rinsed~zero ox
  • handfulFresh dill fronds~zero ox
  • 1Lemon, wedged for serving~zero ox
🫓 Method
  1. 1
    Preheat oven to 400°F
    Line a baking sheet with parchment. Prepare a large shallow dish of warm water.
  2. 2
    Soak and stack 4 sheets
    Dip one sheet ~15 seconds — just pliable. Lay flat on parchment. Repeat for 3 more sheets, layering directly on top. The stack will bond as it sits.
  3. 3
    Roll and form the ring
    Roll the 4-layer stack tightly from one edge into a log. Join both ends to form a ring. Press the seam firmly for 10 seconds — the tacky surface holds without anything else.
  4. 4
    Oil, top, and bake
    Brush or spray the outside lightly with olive oil. Press the garlic/onion/herb topping mix onto the outside. Bake 18–22 minutes until golden and blistered.
  5. 5
    Cool 3–4 min on a rack
    The outside crisps further off the heat. Slice horizontally.
  6. 6
    Load and serve
    Spread labneh on both cut faces. Layer smoked salmon, cucumber, capers, dill. Finish with a squeeze of lemon.
🔄 Variations — same technique, different fills
  • Avocado + poached egg: The bagel holds a sliced avocado and egg beautifully. Avocado is APOE4-controversial in large amounts but half an avocado on a high-fat protein breakfast is within range — the monounsaturated fat and lutein are beneficial.
  • Labneh + roasted cherry tomatoes + fresh basil: Lower protein, more Mediterranean. The acidity of the tomatoes against the labneh tang is very good. Roast the tomatoes separately at 400°F for 15 min while the bagels bake.
  • Cream cheese substitute — any nut-based spread: Cashew cream (note: cashews are moderately high oxalate — ~49mg per 30g). Macadamia-based spreads are very low oxalate and work well.
  • Air fryer version: 380°F for 12–15 minutes. The outside crisps faster. Watch at the 10-minute mark — air fryers vary significantly. Result is slightly more uniform than oven; oven gives better surface blistering.
  • Make-ahead shells: Baked shells store well in an airtight container for 2 days at room temperature. Do not refrigerate — moisture from the fridge softens the crisp exterior. Reload with toppings just before eating.

🩸 GL per bagel shell ~5 (low). The high-fat labneh + protein from salmon slow glucose absorption substantially — safe for the DP window and as a general breakfast.
