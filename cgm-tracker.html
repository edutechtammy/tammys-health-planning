📡 CGM Cycle Tracker

~2-week Lingo sensor cycles · Time-in-range · Spikes & hypo events · Cross-cycle trends tied to each 10% weight-loss milestone

Δ G = ( C ⋅ C F ) × M order × M time × M recipe × M bio × M activity

∆G (Target Value): The predicted peak blood sugar rise (change from baseline) in mg/dL

C (Carbs): Total net carbohydrates in the meal (grams).

CF (Carb Factor): Your personalized baseline mg/dL rise per gram of carbohydrate under perfect conditions. To calculate this, you need a single ingredient food with precise grams and the carbohydrate count without fiber.

Morder (Food Order Multiplier): A fractional discount based on sequencing.

Mtime (Time of Day / Dawn Phenomenon Multiplier): Adjusts for your diurnal insulin sensitivity.

Mrecipe (Recipe Structure Multiplier): Adjusts for the state of foods in the meal (liquid, highly processed, raw, etc.).

Mbio (Biological Multiplier): Adjusts for lifestyle factors (1 is a perfect day while a higher number is a high stress low sleep day).

Mactivity (Activity Multiplier): Adjusts for physical activity levels where a 1 is a sedentary day and lower numbers are active days such as 0.8 for a casual 15-minute walk immediately after eating.


The equation predicts the magnitude of the highest peak, not the exact minute the peak occurs.

The prediction will be more accurate with fasting blood sugar because the amount of circulating insulin is more stable.

💉 Fingerstick Pre-Meal Decision Guide — Between CGM Cycles

You confirmed in Cycle 1 that you cannot feel hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia. This guide translates your CGM data into fingerstick decision thresholds so you can make the same informed call — eat now vs. wait vs. choose something different — even when the sensor isn't on. All thresholds are derived from Cycle 1 data and will refine as future cycles add more food response measurements.

🔴 Universal Floor — Applies to Every Reading, Every Time of Day

🚨 Below 85 mg/dL — Eat Now

Do not factor in what you planned to eat. Choose any protein + fat snack immediately (yogurt, cheese, eggs, meat).

Do not wait for hunger. Do not choose by preference. Cycle 1 showed lows can start from readings that feel completely normal.

✓ 85–95 mg/dL — All Clear

Full green light for any food including higher-carb items. No restrictions, no modifications needed.

This is your ideal pre-meal window — glucose is stable, insulin sensitivity is at a good baseline.

⏰ Morning Modifier (before 10 AM) — Be 10 Points Stricter on All Thresholds Your cortisol awakening response (CAR) pushes glucose upward from the moment you decide to get up — before any food. A 95 mg/dL reading at 8 AM will be ~105 by the time you take the first bite. Cycle 1 measured your pure CAR ceiling at 123 mg/dL (Jun 4, no food at all). Subtract 10 from every green-light ceiling below if the time is before 10 AM.

🟡 Yellow Zone Protocol — When You Want the Higher-Carb Food Anyway

Food sequencing changes the absorption curve — no retest needed

  1. Eat 3–4 bites of a protein or fat item first (eggs, cheese, meat, yogurt)
  2. Wait 5–10 minutes before starting the carb portion
  3. Use ACV dressing on the meal if it's available
  4. Proceed — the sequencing has already blunted the absorption rate

This protocol is based on Cycle 1's Jun 6 result: same breakfast as Jun 1 but with Chop Chop Salad (ACV dressing) → peak 148 for 20 min vs. 166 for 60 min.

🌙 Bedtime — Non-Optional Rule

✓ Always eat bedtime yogurt or kefir — not optional on exercise nights

Cycle 1 directly tested skipping it (May 28): result was a 185-minute low from 23:44 through early AM, nadir 60 mg/dL during sleep.

On Zone 2 exercise nights (target HR reached): increase add-ins — add walnuts or increase portion. The Jun 4→5 delayed hypos (55 mg/dL overnight, then 59 PM next day) followed the first two target-HR sessions.

Bedtime reading < 100: Full yogurt/kefir portion + walnuts or protein add-in.
Bedtime reading 100–120: Standard yogurt/kefir portion.
Bedtime reading > 120: Plain kefir only (lower carb) or a small cheese portion.

These thresholds are derived from Cycle 1 (May 27 – Jun 7, 2026) with no ACV and ~1.5–2.3 MPH treadmill walking. They will be updated as Cycle 2 and beyond refine the expected rise values for each food category and confirm the ACV effect size.
