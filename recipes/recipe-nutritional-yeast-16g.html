Nutritional Yeast (2 Tbsp / 16 g Serving)

A stand-alone nutrient card for fortified nutritional yeast. It is one of the most useful low-fat, low-carb, high-B12 seasonings in this plan and fits the APOE4, fatty liver, and MTHFR strategy very well.

Serving size: 2 Tbsp / 16 g Calories: ~60 kcal Protein: 8.0 g Vitamin B12: 5–10 mcg

Protein is complete, with all 9 essential amino acids. B12 and folate vary by fortification brand, so label-checking matters.

Why this matters in your protocol
  • Complete protein: 8 g per 2 tbsp serving, with all 9 essential amino acids.
  • APOE4 / fatty liver friendly: only 0.1 g saturated fat and almost no MUFA or inflammatory load.
  • MTHFR / methylation support: B12 can be especially valuable when fortified, and folate contributes to methylation support.
  • Low glycemic load: about 2 g net carbs and ~1.5 g viscous soluble fiber with beta-glucan benefits.
  • Immune-supportive fiber: beta-glucans contribute to gut and immune resilience without pushing blood sugar.
