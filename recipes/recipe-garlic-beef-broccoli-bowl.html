Garlic Beef & Broccoli Bowl

A rapid skillet meal using lean beef and broccoli to create a dense choline bowl with liver-supportive sulfur compounds and a very low glycemic load.

⏱ 18 min total 🥩 150g lean beef 🥦 150g broccoli 🌿 GF / grain-free
APOE4 Celiac / GF DP / Blood Sugar Fatty Liver MTHFR Kidney Stone
Ingredients
  • 150glean beef steak, sliced thin
  • 150gfresh broccoli florets, cut small
  • 15gextra virgin olive oil, divided
  • 12gfresh garlic cloves, crushed
  • 45gwater or clean beef bone broth
  • 5gcoconut aminos or gluten-free tamari
  • to tastesea salt and cracked black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1
    Prep components
    Slice the beef very thin and crush the garlic, letting it rest for 10 minutes.
  2. 2
    Sear the beef
    Heat half the olive oil in a hot skillet. Cook the beef until browned but still tender, then remove it to a clean plate.
  3. 3
    Steam-sauté broccoli
    Add the remaining oil, toss in the broccoli, then add the water or broth and cover for 2–3 minutes until tender-crisp.
  4. 4
    Aromatize
    Push the broccoli aside, add the garlic, and cook 30 seconds until fragrant.
  5. 5
    Finish
    Stir in the coconut aminos, return the beef and juices, and toss for 1 minute until everything is glossy and hot.
