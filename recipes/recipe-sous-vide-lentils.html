Sous Vide Lentils (12 oz Jar)

Overnight sous vide red lentils cooked in a mason jar at precise low temperature, creating a smooth, velvety paste perfect for meal prep. The gentle cooking preserves nutrients while breaking down anti-nutrients for better digestion and blood sugar management.

⏱ 10 min prep + 8 hours cook 🍽 1.5 cup jar ❄️ Keeps 5 days refrigerated
DP / Blood Sugar Celiac / GF
🥗 Ingredients & Method
🧾 Ingredients — 1.5 cup jar
  • 0.33 cup dry red lentils (rinsed)
  • 0.66 cup water or vegetable broth
  • 1 small garlic clove (smashed)
  • 1 splash extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 pinch salt
🔪 Method — 8 hours total
  1. 1
    Combine ingredients
    Combine rinsed red lentils, liquid, garlic, olive oil, and salt directly into a 1.5-cup (12 oz) wide-mouth mason jar. Stir gently.
  2. 2
    Seal jar
    Wipe the rim of the jar clean. Apply the flat disc lid and screw the metal band on strictly fingertip-tight.
  3. 3
    Place in water bath
    Place the jar inside the multi-cooker pot, keeping the central bottom sensor clear. Fill the pot with water up to the jar's shoulders.
  4. 4
    Cook sous vide
    Program the Sous Vide function to 180°F (82.2°C) for 8 hours.
  5. 5
    Finish
    In the morning, carefully remove the jar. Once slightly cooled, stir vigorously or pulse with a blender until it forms a smooth, velvety paste.

🩸 Low glycemic — Red lentils provide resistant starch and fiber for steady blood sugar
