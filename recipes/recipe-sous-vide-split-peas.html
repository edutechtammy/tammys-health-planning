Overnight Sous Vide Green Split Peas in Broth (12 oz Jar)

Rinsed green split peas cooked overnight in gluten-free bone broth with garlic, bay leaf, thyme, and black pepper. The gentle sous vide cooking breaks down the peas into a thick, smooth, savory paste while preserving the mineral-rich broth nutrients.

⏱ 10 min prep + 8 hours cook 🍽 1.5 cup jar ❄️ Keeps 5 days refrigerated
DP / Blood Sugar Celiac / GF
🥗 Ingredients & Method
🧾 Ingredients — 1.5 cup jar
  • 0.33 cup dry green split peas (rinsed)
  • 0.83 cup gluten-free chicken or vegetable bone broth
  • 1 small garlic clove (smashed)
  • 1 small bay leaf
  • 1 pinch dried thyme
  • 1 pinch black pepper
🔪 Method — 8 hours total
  1. 1
    Combine ingredients
    Combine rinsed split peas, gluten-free broth, smashed garlic, bay leaf, thyme, and black pepper in the 12 oz mason jar. Do not add salt before cooking.
  2. 2
    Seal jar
    Wipe the rim clean and seal the flat lid down with the screw band using only fingertip pressure.
  3. 3
    Place in water bath
    Place the jar into the multi-cooker pot water bath, ensuring the water level matches the shoulders of the jar.
  4. 4
    Cook sous vide
    Program the Sous Vide cycle to 180°F (82.2°C) for 8 hours.
  5. 5
    Finish
    In the morning, carefully pull the jar out, fish out the bay leaf, and mash or blend the collapsed peas into a thick, smooth, savory paste. Add salt to taste.

🩸 Low glycemic — Split peas provide resistant starch and soluble fiber; bone broth adds protein for blood sugar stability
