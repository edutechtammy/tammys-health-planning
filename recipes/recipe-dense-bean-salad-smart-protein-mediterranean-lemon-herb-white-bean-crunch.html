The "Smart Protein" Mediterranean Lemon-Herb & White Bean Crunch Salad

A high-protein dense bean salad with cannellini beans, nutritional yeast, hemp hearts, crunchy greens, and a lemon-herb dressing. It gives you dense protein, strong fiber, and nearly no saturated fat, which is exactly the profile you want for a Mediterranean, APOE4-friendly lunch bowl.

⏱ Prep: 10 min 🍽 Yield: 1 large serving 🥗 Smart Protein
APOE4 Celiac / GF DP / Blood Sugar Fatty Liver MTHFR
Low GI • protein-dense and fiber-forward

Smart Protein check: 19.5g protein, 0.3g saturated fat, and a 65:1 protein-to-sat-fat ratio.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cupcannellini beans
  • 2 tbspnutritional yeast
  • 1 tbsphemp hearts
  • 1.5 cupsshredded cabbage or romaine
  • 1/2 cupcucumber, diced
  • 1/4 cupred bell pepper, diced
  • 2 tbspfresh parsley + mint
  • 1.5 tspEVOO
  • 1.5 tbspfresh lemon juice
  • 1/2 tspDijon mustard
  • to tastegarlic powder, oregano, salt, black pepper
Method
  1. 1
    Whisk the dressing
    Whisk the lemon juice, EVOO, Dijon, garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper in the salad bowl until emulsified.
  2. 2
    Coat the beans
    Add the cannellini beans and toss so they absorb the lemon-herb dressing while you prep the vegetables.
  3. 3
    Add the protein
    Stir in the nutritional yeast and hemp hearts until the bean mixture becomes savory and thick.
  4. 4
    Toss the crunch
    Add the cabbage, cucumber, bell pepper, parsley, and mint. Toss thoroughly until evenly coated.
  5. 5
    Serve
    Enjoy immediately as a crisp lunch or protein-dense meal bowl.

Why it fits

  • Dense bean + fiber structure gives long satiety without pushing saturated fat over your limit.
  • Nutritional yeast adds complete protein and B12, while the legumes deliver the base fiber and fullness.
  • EVOO and the raw greens keep the meal Mediterranean, anti-inflammatory, and very easy to repeat.
