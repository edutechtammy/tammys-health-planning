Lemony White Bean, Kale & Herb Protein Salad

A softly massaged kale salad with cannellini beans, lemon, herbs, and nutritional yeast. It is dense, fiber-forward, and high in protein while staying extremely light on saturated fat and easy to repeat for lunch.

⏱ Prep: 12 min 🍽 Yield: 1 large serving 🥗 Smart Protein
APOE4 Celiac / GF DP / Blood Sugar Fatty Liver MTHFR
Low GI • slow-digesting and satisfying

Smart Protein check: 18.4g protein, 0.4g saturated fat, and a 46:1 protein-to-sat-fat ratio.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cupcannellini beans
  • 2 cupschopped kale
  • 2 tbspnutritional yeast
  • 1 tbsphemp hearts
  • 1/2 cupcucumber, diced
  • 1/4 cupred onion, thinly sliced
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
  • 1 tbspdill, chopped
  • 1.5 tbsplemon juice
  • 1.5 tspEVOO
  • 1/2 tspDijon mustard
  • to tastegarlic powder, salt, black pepper
Method
  1. 1
    Massage the kale
    Massage chopped kale with a little lemon juice and a drizzle of EVOO until it softens slightly.
  2. 2
    Whisk the dressing
    Combine the remaining lemon juice, EVOO, Dijon, garlic powder, salt, and pepper until smooth.
  3. 3
    Combine
    Add the cannellini beans, nutritional yeast, hemp hearts, cucumber, onion, parsley, and dill and toss well.
  4. 4
    Finish
    Fold in the kale and toss until every piece is coated and the bowl feels full and crunchy.

Why it fits

  • Kale adds leafy folate and big texture while the beans provide almost all of the protein.
  • The meal is lean and high-volume, which helps satiety without pushing saturated fat upward.
  • It is ideal when you need a meal that feels hearty but still fits a Mediterranean low-fat pattern.
