Pan-Seared Mackerel with Roasted Cruciferous Medley

Wild mackerel seared in a hot skillet and served with garlic-roasted broccoli. It is one of the cleanest seafood options for brain support, liver support, and true marine omega-3 density. Calories: 500 kcals/serving.

Ingredients
  • 150gwild mackerel fillet, thawed and dried
  • 120gbroccoli florets, cut small
  • 15gextra virgin olive oil, divided
  • 12ggarlic cloves, crushed
  • to tastesea salt if needed; pouch may already contain salt
Instructions
  1. 1
    Roast the medley
    Preheat the oven to 400°F and toss the broccoli with half the olive oil and crushed garlic. Roast 12 to 15 minutes until crisp at the edges.
  2. 2
    Prep the fish
    Pat the mackerel very dry so the skin sears properly instead of steaming.
  3. 3
    Sear
    Heat the remaining olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat. Lay the fish skin-side down, lower to medium, and press gently for the first 30 seconds.
  4. 4
    Finish
    Cook 3 to 4 minutes until the skin is crisp and golden, then flip to the flesh side for 60 to 90 seconds more.
  5. 5
    Serve
    Plate with the roasted broccoli and spoon over the pan oil and garlic bits.
