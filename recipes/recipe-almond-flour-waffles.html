Almond Flour Waffles

A gluten-free waffle built from almond flour, eggs, milk, and monkfruit-allulose sweetener. This is a practical warm-weather breakfast because it uses a waffle iron instead of heating a full oven.

Prep: 10 min Cook: 12-15 min Yield: about 6 waffles Net carbs: ~7g per waffle Saturated fat: ~4g per waffle

Macro note: estimates assume 6 waffles total and can vary with milk choice and exact sweetener blend.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups almond flour
  • 3/4 cup monkfruit-allulose 1:1 sugar replacement
  • 3 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 1/2 cups milk
  • 1/3 cup almond oil
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
Kidney stone note: almond flour is moderate-to-higher oxalate compared with many flour alternatives, so portion size and calcium pairing matter.

Method

Waffle iron timing: start preheating when your batter is almost ready, based on how fast your machine heats. For fast-heating irons, this prevents the plates from overheating or smoking while you mix.
  1. Whisk almond flour, monkfruit-allulose sweetener, and baking powder in a large bowl.
  2. In another bowl, whisk eggs, milk, almond oil, and vanilla extract until smooth.
  3. Pour wet into dry and mix until no dry pockets remain.
  4. Rest batter 3 to 5 minutes; if batter is too thick, add 1 to 2 tablespoons milk.
  5. Preheat and lightly grease your waffle iron.
  6. Cook in batches until golden and set.
