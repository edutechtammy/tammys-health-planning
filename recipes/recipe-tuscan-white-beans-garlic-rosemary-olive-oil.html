Tuscan White Beans with Garlic, Rosemary & Olive Oil

Warm, buttery cannellini or great northern beans are gently cooked in a skillet with garlic, rosemary, tomato, and extra virgin olive oil. The garlic is smashed rather than minced so the oil infuses beautifully without scorching or turning bitter. The result is comforting and rustic, with a soft bean texture and classic Tuscan flavor that feels familiar from the very first bite.

⏱ Prep: 5 min 🔥 Cook: 15 min ⏳ Total: ~20 min 🍽 Servings: 2 to 3

🥘 The easiest way to make it taste right: Don’t rush the garlic and rosemary in the oil — that brief sauté is what gives the whole dish its flavor foundation.

🧾 Ingredients — 2 to 3 servings
  • 2 cansCannellini or Great Northern beans🟢 low ox
  • 3 tbspExtra virgin olive oil, divided🟢 EVOO
  • 3–4 clovesGarlic, smashed and peeled⬜ zero
  • 1 sprigFresh rosemary (or 1 tsp dried)⬜ zero
  • 1 sprigFresh thyme, optional⬜ zero
  • pinchRed pepper flakes⬜ zero
  • to tasteSalt and black pepper⬜ zero
  • 1 cupCrushed tomatoes or tomato sauce🟢 low ox
  • serve withCrusty whole-grain bread or lemon juice🟢 pairing
👩‍🍳 Instructions
  1. 1
    Infuse the oil
    Heat 2 tablespoons of extra virgin olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the smashed garlic cloves, fresh rosemary, and a pinch of red pepper flakes. Sauté for 2 to 3 minutes until the garlic is golden and the oil is fragrant. Smashing the garlic flavors the oil without burning tiny minced pieces.
  2. 2
    Simmer the tomatoes
    Stir in the crushed tomatoes and bring to a gentle simmer for 3 to 4 minutes so the flavors can meld together.
  3. 3
    Add the beans
    Dump in the drained white beans, stirring gently so you do not crush them. Season with a generous pinch of salt and black pepper.
  4. 4
    Gently cook
    Turn the heat to medium-low and let the mixture simmer gently for 8 to 10 minutes until the beans are warmed through and have absorbed the sauce.
  5. 5
    Serve
    Remove the rosemary stem and garlic cloves or leave them in if you like roasted garlic. Spoon into shallow bowls, drizzle the remaining 1 tablespoon of olive oil on top, and serve with warm toasted whole-grain bread.

💡 Why this works for beginners

  • Ultra-satisfying texture: cannellini beans have a velvety interior that feels rich and comforting without being heavy.
  • Simple, familiar flavors: garlic, tomato, olive oil, and rosemary are classic and approachable from the first bite.
  • Versatile: eat this as a standalone lunch, spoon it over grilled chicken or fish for dinner, or enjoy it with toasted bread like an Italian bruschetta.
