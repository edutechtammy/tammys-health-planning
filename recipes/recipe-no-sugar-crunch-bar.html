No-Sugar Crunch Bar

A dense, crunchy seed-and-nut bar that uses psyllium husk and ground flax as the binder, so it stays sturdy without added sugar, grain flour, or any sticky sweeteners. It is naturally grain-free and gluten-free, and the ingredients provide a satisfying snack texture with a much lower glucose load than typical granola bars.

⏱ 15 min prep ❄️ 2 hours to set (or 15 min bake) 🍽 8 bars 🫙 Refrigerate 5-7 days
Celiac / GF DP / Blood Sugar Kidney Stone
Net carbs: ~3–4 g/bar Saturated fat: ~2–3 g/bar

Ingredients

  • 1/2 cup + 2 tbspsmooth unsweetened almond butter (145g), slightly warmed
  • 1/2 cuppepitas (pumpkin seeds), pre-salted
  • 1/2 cuphemp hearts (80g)
  • 1/2 cupalmonds, roughly chopped (70g)
  • 2 tbspground flaxseed (20g)
  • 2 tbsppsyllium husk (whole) (10g)
  • 4 tbspwarm water (60ml)
  • 1/2 tspcinnamon
  • 1 tspvanilla extract, optional and only if baking
Pepitas are pre-salted here; do not add extra salt. The texture depends on the psyllium and flax gel setting properly.

Method

  1. 1Activate the binder: Whisk the psyllium husk, ground flaxseed, and warm water together in a small bowl. Let it sit for 5 minutes until it thickens into a gel. This is the glue that makes the bars hold together without a sugar syrup or starch-based binder.
  2. 2Combine the dry mix: In a large bowl, mix the pepitas, hemp hearts, chopped almonds, and cinnamon. Keep the mixture fairly even so every bar has a consistent crunch.
  3. 3Bring it together: Stir in the warmed almond butter and the psyllium-flax gel. Mix until the texture becomes a thick, sticky dough with no dry pockets. If baking, blend the vanilla into the warm almond butter before combining.
  4. 4Press and set: Line an 8x4-inch loaf pan with parchment. Press the mixture in very firmly—compact it well so the bars hold their shape. For the no-bake method, chill 2 hours before slicing. For the baked method, bake at 325°F (160°C) for 15 minutes, cool completely, and then slice. Keep refrigerated for the best texture.

Choose your finish

No-Bake

Press firmly into the loaf pan, refrigerate 2 hours, then slice into 8 bars. This method keeps the crunch and avoids baking the seeds and nuts too much.

Baked

Use vanilla only if baking. Bake at 325°F (160°C) for 15 minutes, then cool fully in the pan before slicing so the bars don't crack apart.

Texture note

For the densest bars, press the mixture as hard as you can before chilling or baking. A loose press makes the bars crumble more easily.

If you want a softer, less crisp bite, use the no-bake method and keep the bars in the fridge. If you want a more set, slightly firmer structure, the 15-minute bake is the better option.

Cookie-like sweet variation

The base version is intentionally savory and crunchy. If you want a sweeter, more cookie-like version with the same seed-and-nut structure, use this variation instead:

  • 2 tbspmonkfruit + allulose blend or another granulated no-sugar sweetener
  • 1 tspvanilla extract
  • 1 tbspunsweetened shredded coconut
  • 1/4 tspcinnamon or a pinch of nutmeg for a warm cookie profile
Mix the sweetener and vanilla into the warm almond butter before combining everything, then press firmly and bake at 325°F (160°C) for 15 minutes. This gives the bars a softer, more cookie-like edge while still staying far lower in carbohydrate impact than a standard granola or cookie bar.

Best eaten as a controlled snack; the nuts and seeds are calorie-dense and the texture is meant to be satisfying without needing a sweet finish.
