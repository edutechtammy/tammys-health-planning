Plain Baked Salmon (40 g Serving)

A simple stand-alone reference card for plain baked salmon. This card focuses on nutrient ranges per 40 g cooked serving with no added oils or sauces.

Serving size: 40 g cooked salmon Prep: 2 min Bake: 10 to 12 min Total: ~14 min
APOE4 Fatty Liver MTHFR DP / Blood Sugar Celiac / GF
Nutrient ranges per 40 g serving
Calories~70 to 85 kcal
Protein~8.5 to 10.5 g
Saturated Fat~0.3 to 0.5 g
Vitamin B12~0.9 to 1.2 mcg
Folate (B9)~1.0 to 3.5 mcg
MUFAs (Monounsaturated Fat)~1.1 to 1.5 g
Omega-3 Fatty Acids~0.6 to 0.9 g
Choline~25 to 35 mg

Ranges are approximate and can vary by salmon species, fat content, and bake loss.

Plain bake method (single portion)
  • Preheat oven to 400F (200C).
  • Place a 40 g salmon portion on parchment.
  • Bake 10 to 12 minutes until flaky and fully cooked.
  • Serve plain, or pair with a non-starchy side as needed.
