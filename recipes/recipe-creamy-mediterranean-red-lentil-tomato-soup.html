Creamy Mediterranean Red Lentil & Tomato Soup

This is an easy, pantry-friendly soup that tastes rich without being heavy. Red lentils simmer down into a naturally creamy texture, while onion, carrots, garlic, and tomato paste create a savory Mediterranean base. The final squeeze of lemon keeps the soup bright and helps the flavors taste more complete, rather than flat or overly sweet.

⏱ Prep: 10 min 🔥 Cook: 20 min ⏳ Total: ~30 min 🍽 Servings: 4
APOE4 Celiac / GF DP / Blood Sugar Fatty Liver MTHFR
Low-to-moderate GI; very gentle, warming, and easy to digest.

👩‍🍳 Beginner-friendly note: No blender is needed. Red lentils break down on their own, so the soup becomes naturally creamy while the vegetables keep a soft, comforting texture.

Why this works: The cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, and tomato paste taste familiar and cozy, like a tomato soup or lentil stew without any sharp or unusual flavors. It is especially useful as a quick, nourishing lunch or a mild, comforting evening meal.
🧾 Ingredients — 4 servings
  • 1 cupDried red lentils🟢 low ox
  • 2 tbspExtra virgin olive oil🟢 EVOO
  • 1Yellow onion, diced⬜ zero
  • 2Carrots, diced🟢 low ox
  • 2 clovesGarlic, minced⬜ zero
  • 2 tbspTomato paste🟢 low ox
  • 1 tspGround cumin⬜ zero
  • 1/2 tspSmoked paprika⬜ zero
  • 1/2 tspDried oregano⬜ zero
  • 4 cupsLow-sodium vegetable or chicken broth⬜ zero
  • 1/2Lemon, juiced⬜ zero
  • to tasteSalt and black pepper⬜ zero
  • optionalFresh parsley for garnish🟢 low ox
  • optionalCrusty whole-grain bread or feta🟢 pairing
👩‍🍳 Instructions
  1. 1
    Sauté the vegetables
    Heat the olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and carrots with a pinch of salt and cook for 5 to 7 minutes until the onion is soft and translucent. Stir in the garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  2. 2
    Toast the spices
    Stir in the tomato paste, cumin, smoked paprika, and oregano. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring constantly. Toasting the tomato paste and spices in oil brings out a deeper, richer savory flavor.
  3. 3
    Add lentils & broth
    Add the rinsed red lentils and pour in the broth. Stir well, scraping up any cooked bits from the bottom of the pot.
  4. 4
    Simmer
    Bring to a boil, then lower the heat to a gentle simmer, cover, and cook for 15 to 20 minutes. The lentils will soften and dissolve into a thick, creamy soup. This is where the naturally velvety texture comes from.
  5. 5
    Brighten & Serve
    Turn off the heat and stir in the lemon juice. Season with salt and black pepper to taste. Serve hot with fresh parsley on top, and add whole-grain bread or feta if desired.

💡 Why this works for beginners

  • No blender needed: red lentils naturally dissolve and thicken the soup as they cook, giving you a creamy texture with almost no extra work.
  • Warm, familiar flavors: the cumin, smoked paprika, and tomato base taste cozy and approachable, like a gentle tomato soup or a quick lentil stew.
  • Good pairing: serve with toasted whole-grain bread brushed with olive oil or sprinkle a little feta on top for extra richness.
  • Don’t skip the lemon: the acid brightens the soup and keeps the flavor from tasting flat or overly heavy.
