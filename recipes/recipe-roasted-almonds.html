Low-and-Slow Roasted Almonds (Toaster Oven)

A reference card for roasting raw skin-on whole almonds at a gentler temperature so you can control ingredient quality and avoid over-roasting.

Prep: ~2 min Roast: 10 to 15 min Temp: 250°F to 275°F (121°C to 135°C) Serving target: 28g
Low-and-slow profile: 250°F to 275°F for 10 to 15 minutes
APOE4 Fatty Liver DP / Blood Sugar Celiac / GF MTHFR
Targeted nutrients — per 28g serving explicit panel values
Choline — APOE4 · Fatty Liver ~14 mg
~3% of daily target
Omega-3 — supporting fatty liver + APOE4 ~5 mg
~0% of therapeutic daily target
Folate — MTHFR ~14 mcg
~4% of daily target
B12 — MTHFR 0 mcg
0% of daily target
Primary value is fat profile support (mostly monounsaturated fat). Use as a controlled-portion snack rather than a high-volume food.
Ingredients — 8 servings
  • 224gskin-on raw whole almonds
  • pinchsea salt (optional)
Instructions
  1. 1
    Preheat toaster oven
    Set toaster oven to 250°F to 275°F (121°C to 135°C).
  2. 2
    Single layer
    Spread almonds on a baking tray in one even layer.
  3. 3
    Roast gently
    Roast 10 to 15 minutes. Check at 10 minutes and pull when aromatic and lightly golden.
  4. 4
    Cool completely
    Cool before storing, then portion into 28g servings for consistent planner use.
