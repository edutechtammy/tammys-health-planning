Marinated Smoked Gouda in Chili & Herb EVOO (Version 2)

Aged smoked Gouda cubed and marinated in raw cold-pressed extra-virgin olive oil with jalapeño, dried chili, oregano, parsley, and lemon zest. It has the smoky luxury of an aged cheese, the heat and brightness of a chili oil, and the aromatic richness of a proper Mediterranean mezze. No cooking, no heat, no fuss — just a small bowl of flavour that feels special while still staying in the low-carbohydrate lane when portioned thoughtfully.

Portion note: This is a luxury version, not a whole-cheese plate. Keep the serving to small cubes and use it as a thoughtful appetizer or side rather than a large snack.
👩‍🍳 Method
  1. 1
    Cube the Gouda
    Cut the aged smoked Gouda into rustic cubes or wedges, roughly 1 inch across. Aged Gouda holds its shape beautifully and gives the jar a silky, luxurious finish without breaking down into a paste.
  2. 2
    Layer the jar
    Place cheese in a clean 500 mL glass jar, layering with jalapeño slices, dried chilies, and oregano between the pieces. Scatter lemon zest through the layers so the floral aromatics are carried through the oil.
  3. 3
    Cover with raw EVOO
    Pour cold-pressed extra-virgin olive oil over the cheese until it is mostly submerged. Do not heat the oil. Raw EVOO matters here because the peppery phenolics are more intact and the finish is cleaner and deeper.
  4. 4
    Add parsley and marinate
    Scatter chopped parsley over the top, seal the jar, and leave it to marinate for 30 minutes minimum. For a richer smoky result, refrigerate overnight and then bring it back to room temperature 30 minutes before serving.
  5. 5
    Serve
    Serve directly from the jar or spoon onto a small plate. This is the more indulgent, deeper version of the original feta jar: smokier, richer, and slightly more luxurious. Serve with cucumber, olives, radishes, or small salad greens. The infused oil is excellent over eggs or vegetables.
What matters here: Raw EVOO preserves peppery polyphenols and oleocanthal more effectively than heated oil. Capsaicin from the jalapeño and dried chili helps slow gastric emptying and may support a steadier glucose response when paired modestly. Aged smoked Gouda brings a deep savory, umami-rich finish while staying low-carb if the portion remains intentional and small.
🗓 Storage: Refrigerate sealed. Keeps for 1–2 weeks. The EVOO will firm up when cold — this is normal. Pull from the fridge about 30 minutes before serving so it becomes liquid again. Use the infused oil as a finishing drizzle; it is practically a condiment at that point.

🍽 Best use

🥒 Mezze plate Pair with cucumber, olives, radishes, and a small side salad. It is a richer, more indulgent bite than feta, so a small plate is the right balance.
🥗 Salad finish Add a few cubes over a leafy salad and drizzle a spoonful of the infused chili oil over the top instead of dressing.
🍳 Egg pairing Spoon over eggs or a warm omelet. The smoky cheese and chili oil add a luxurious finishing note without requiring extra carbs.
🌿 Milder version Reduce to one dried chili and skip the jalapeño if your gut is reactive that day. The oil still carries the herb and olive flavor without the stronger heat load.

🔬 Why this version works

  • Smoked Gouda has a richer finish: It brings a more luxurious, smoky, nutty flavor than feta while still fitting the low-carb pattern when served in a small portion.
  • Raw EVOO is still the key: Heat degrades the peppery phenolics and reduces the benefit of the oil as a functional ingredient. This is not just a garnish — it is part of the marinade.
  • Capsaicin stays purposeful: The jalapeño and dried chili are not there for drama alone; they add heat and insulin-sensitivity support in a small, controlled amount.
  • Parsley keeps the freshness: It adds brightness and some folate without cooking it down or muddying the flavor. This keeps the jar vibrant rather than heavy.
  • Serve as a small bite: This is the richer, more indulgent cousin to the feta version. The delicious part is the intensity — so keep it deliberate and do not turn it into a large cheese board.
