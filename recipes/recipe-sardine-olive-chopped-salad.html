Sardine & Olive Chopped Salad

A large chopped salad built around sardines, cucumber, romaine, Greek olive salad, parsley, and EVOO. It is designed for deep marine omega-3 coverage, strong choline support, and a very flat post-meal glucose curve.

⏱ Prep: 10 min 🍽 1 large serving 🐟 Sardines: 120g 📉 Net carbs: ~4g
APOE4 Celiac / GF DP / Blood Sugar Fatty Liver MTHFR Kidney Stone
Ingredients
The Salad
  • 120gsardines, packed in EVOO or water, drained
  • 200gEnglish cucumber, finely diced
  • 75gromaine or mixed iceberg greens, chopped
  • 30gItalian olive salad or chopped Kalamata olives
  • 15ghemp hearts
  • 10gfresh parsley, chopped
  • 15gextra virgin olive oil
  • optionaltiny pinch of Celtic sea salt and lemon squeeze
Instructions
  1. 1
    Prep the greens
    Combine the chopped romaine and finely diced cucumber in a large bowl.
  2. 2
    Add texture and herbs
    Fold in the hemp hearts and parsley so the salad has body and brightness.
  3. 3
    Add the olive salad
    Stir in the olive salad so the seasoned oil and brine coat the greens and cucumber.
  4. 4
    Flake the sardines
    Open the sardines and gently break them into bite-sized pieces, then fold them into the salad.
  5. 5
    Finish and serve
    Drizzle in the EVOO, add a tiny squeeze of lemon if desired, and toss gently so the fish stays intact but is evenly coated.
