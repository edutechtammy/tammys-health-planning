Fresh Parsley (4 g Serving)

This tiny serving is almost negligible in calories, but it delivers the kind of clean folate and choline support that makes a good garnish genuinely useful. It also adds a trace of viscous fiber without pushing net carbs up.

Total carbs are roughly 0.37 g and fiber is about 0.13 g, leaving a net carb value of about 0.25 g. Folate is a clean, effortless methylation boost for a very small serving size.

Why it earns a spot
  • Very low calorie load: just 1.4 kcal per 4 g.
  • Useful folate contribution without needing a big serving.
  • Trace viscous fiber and negligible net carbs.
  • Zero B12, but a clean herb addition that fits a low-fat, low-carb garnish strategy.
