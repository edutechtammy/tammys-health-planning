Ceylon Cinnamon and Psyllium Mints

A small no-sugar mint made from monkfruit/allulose, Ceylon cinnamon, and psyllium husk powder. This is a quick prep recipe with long air-curing time. Cinnamon and psyllium are both used as blood-sugar support ingredients, but portion size must stay small.

⏱ 10-12 min prep 🍬 About 60 small mints 🌬 Air cure 24-48 hours
DP / Blood Sugar Celiac / GF Kidney Stone

Warning

No more than two small mints in close time succession. Psyllium swells and forms a gel, which can become a choking and digestive-system hazard.

Use this as a small adjunct, not a snack replacement. Track your response in Food Testing if you use these around carb-containing meals.

🧾 Ingredients
  • 1 cupMonkfruit/allulose sweetener blend
  • 1 tspPsyllium husk powder
  • 2 tspCeylon cinnamon powder
  • 2-3 tbspWater
👩‍🍳 Method
  1. 1
    Combine dry mix
    Add sweetener, psyllium powder, and Ceylon cinnamon to a bowl. Stir until uniform so the psyllium and cinnamon are evenly distributed.
  2. 2
    Hydrate slowly
    Add 2 tablespoons water and knead. Add up to 1 more tablespoon only if needed. Stop when the dough is cohesive and clay-like, not sticky.
  3. 3
    Shape cylinder
    Knead into a narrow cylinder, similar to a slim clay rope.
  4. 4
    Slice mints
    Slice into small, even mint pieces. Keep each piece small to support safer serving size control.
  5. 5
    Air cure
    Lay pieces out on parchment with space between them. Air cure for at least 24 to 48 hours until dry and firm.
  6. 6
    Store
    Store fully cured pieces in a clean reused mint tin.

Use Notes

For this recipe, use Ceylon cinnamon specifically (not Cassia). Keep mint size small and dosage conservative. If the pieces are still soft in the center after 24 hours, continue curing to 48 hours before storing.
