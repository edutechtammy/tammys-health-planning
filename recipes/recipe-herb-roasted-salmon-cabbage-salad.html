Herb-Roasted Salmon with Cabbage & Lemon Salad

Roasted salmon paired with a crisp lemon-cabbage salad and a little EVOO. This is the highest-value seafood option for your target list: strong marine omega-3s, high choline, B12, and a clean low-carb profile without avocado or grains.

⏱ 30 min total 🐟 2 salmon fillets 🍽 2 servings 🌿 GF / grain-free

Ingredients
  • 2 filletssalmon, about 5–6 oz each
  • 1 tbspcold-pressed extra virgin olive oil
  • 1 tbspfresh lemon juice
  • 1 tspdried dill or parsley
  • 1/2 headcabbage, thinly shredded
  • 1 cupcucumber, sliced
  • 1 tbspchopped parsley
  • pinchsea salt and black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1
    Make the salad
    Toss the cabbage and cucumber with lemon juice, a little EVOO, chopped parsley, and a pinch of salt. Let it sit 5–10 minutes to soften slightly.
  2. 2
    Roast the salmon
    Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C). Place the salmon on a lined tray. Drizzle with EVOO and lemon, season with dill or parsley, salt, and pepper, then roast 12–15 minutes until just cooked through.
  3. 3
    Serve
    Plate the salmon with the cabbage salad and spoon extra lemon-herb dressing over the top.
