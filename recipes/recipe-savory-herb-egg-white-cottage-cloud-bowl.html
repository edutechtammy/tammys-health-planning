Savory Herb Egg White & Cottage Cheese Cloud Bowl

A fast, high-protein cloud scramble built from liquid egg whites, cottage cheese, chopped greens, and herbs. The curds stay soft and moist when cooked low and slow, giving a training-focused bowl that pushes protein up while keeping net carbs and saturated fat in range.

⏱ Prep 5 min 🔥 Cook 5-7 min 🍽 1 generous training serving

🧾 Ingredients - 1 serving
  • 1 cupliquid egg whites (~240 ml)🟢 low
  • 1/3 cupDaisy Pure & Natural 4% cottage cheese (~75 g)🟢 low
  • 20 gfresh spinach or kale, finely chopped🟢 low
  • 1 tbspfresh chives or dill, finely chopped🟢 low
  • 2 tbspunsweetened skim milk🟢 low
  • 1/2 tspextra virgin olive oil🟢 low
🔪 Method - 10 to 12 minutes total
  1. 1
    Whip the Base
    Whisk egg whites, skim milk, chopped spinach, and herbs until slightly frothy.
  2. 2
    Fold in Curds
    Gently fold in cottage cheese so curd pockets stay intact.
  3. 3
    Cook Low and Slow
    Heat non-stick skillet over medium-low with EVOO and pour mixture into pan.
  4. 4
    Scramble Softly
    After 30 seconds, pull curds slowly with a spatula until just set, 5 to 7 minutes.
  5. 5
    Serve
    Top with extra fresh herbs and black pepper. Serve immediately.

🍽 Serving ideas and combinations

  • Training plate: Pair with cucumber and romaine for extra volume with very low carb impact.
  • Texture tip: Keep heat medium-low and stop cooking while slightly glossy to avoid dryness.
  • Protein stacking: Use this as a base meal when the day still needs a large protein catch-up.
