Turkey & Hemp Heart Lettuce Wraps with Ginger-Tamari Sauce

Lean ground turkey and hemp hearts create a high-protein, low-saturated-fat filling with fresh ginger, activated garlic, and gluten-free tamari. Wrapped in crisp lettuce instead of grain, this is designed as a salad-builder meal that helps you raise protein without spending too much of your daily saturated fat budget.

⏱ Prep 10 min 🔥 Cook 10-12 min 🍽 2 generous servings

🧬 What each ingredient is doing

🦃 Lean turkey Delivers dense complete protein with lower saturated fat than many red-meat options.
🌿 Hemp hearts Adds extra protein, magnesium, and unsaturated fats while keeping carbs low.
🧄 Garlic + ginger Supports anti-inflammatory flavor base and improves meal quality without sugary sauces.
🥬 Lettuce wraps Replaces grain wrappers with a crisp, low-carb structure for DP-friendly assembly.
🧾 Ingredients - 2 servings
  • 250 g lean ground turkey ⬜ ~0 mg
  • 3 tbsp hemp hearts (~30 g) 🟢 low
  • 1 tbsp gluten-free tamari ⬜ ~0 mg
  • 1 tbsp fresh ginger, minced ⬜ ~0 mg
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced (rest 5 min) ⬜ ~0 mg
  • 1 tsp sesame oil ⬜ ~0 mg
  • 3 stalks scallions, chopped 🟢 low
  • 6-8 leaves romaine or butter lettuce, washed and dried 🟢 low
🔪 Method - about 22 minutes
  1. 1
    Activate Aromatics
    Mince garlic and let it rest at least 5 minutes. Prep ginger, scallions, and lettuce leaves.
  2. 2
    Brown Turkey
    Heat skillet over medium-high heat. Add turkey, break apart, and cook 6-8 minutes until browned.
  3. 3
    Build Flavor Base
    Reduce to medium heat, stir in garlic and ginger, and cook about 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. 4
    Sauce + Hemp Hearts
    Add tamari and sesame oil. Stir in hemp hearts and cook 2 minutes so they absorb flavor.
  5. 5
    Finish + Wrap
    Remove from heat, fold in most scallions, and reserve some for garnish. Spoon into lettuce leaves and serve.

🍽 Serving ideas and combinations

  • Salad builder: Add cucumber, herbs, or a squeeze of lemon for a larger low-carb bowl.
  • Protein boost: Use as a topper over chopped romaine when protein target is behind.
  • Batch prep: Cook filling ahead and reheat gently; assemble wraps fresh to preserve crunch.

🩸 Low glycemic profile - high protein with low net carbs; suitable for blood sugar testing as a low-spike meal.
