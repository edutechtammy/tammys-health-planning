Olive & Cheddar Cheese Bread (GF Yeasted Mini Loaf)

A protein-rich gluten-free mini loaf built from a make-ahead base mix of almond flour, sorghum, and psyllium. Each batch uses one egg, active yeast, millet flour, freshly ground flax seed, Italian olive salad (provides healthy fats), and a three-cheddar blend. Baked in a 4-inch mini Pullman pan to create a dense, sliceable bread suitable for sandwiches or toast. The almond flour provides superior protein and healthy fats compared to empty-carb tapioca, though it raises the oxalate level to moderate — pair servings with calcium-rich foods.

⏱ Prep: 15 min + rise 🔥 Bake: 40 min ⏳ Total: ~100 min (incl. 45-60 min rise) 🍽 Yield: 1 mini loaf (4-6 slices) 🌡 325°F / 165°C

👩‍🍳 Instructions
  1. 1
    Make the base mix (once for 3 batches)
    In a jar or airtight container, combine 20g psyllium husk powder, 125g almond flour, 105g sorghum flour, 15g sugar, 5g baking powder, and 6g salt. Shake or stir thoroughly. Label and store at room temp for up to 2 weeks or refrigerate for longer. This makes enough for 3 mini loaves.
  2. 2
    Activate the garlic
    Crush or finely chop 3 garlic cloves and set aside for 5 minutes before using. This allows alliinase to convert alliin into allicin — the active therapeutic compound that supports AMPK activation and glutathione synthesis. Don't skip this step.
  3. 3
    Preheat & prep pan
    Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Lightly grease a Chefmade mini Pullman loaf pan (4×4×3.9 inches, 0.55lb capacity) or similar mini loaf pan with oil or cooking spray.
  4. 4
    Bloom the yeast
    Warm 55g milk to about 110°F / 43°C (not hot — just pleasantly warm). In a small bowl, combine the warm milk with 1 Tbsp active dry yeast. Stir gently and let stand 5-10 minutes until foamy and bubbly. If it doesn't foam, the yeast may be dead — start over with fresh yeast.
  5. 5
    Grind flax & combine dry
    Using a mortar and pestle, grind 1 Tbsp whole flax seeds just before using (maximizes omega-3 freshness). In a mixing bowl, combine 85g prepared base mix, 30g millet flour, the ground flax, 1 tsp chopped fresh rosemary, ½ tsp dried oregano, and black pepper to taste.
  6. 6
    Combine wet & fold
    To the dry mixture, add the bloomed yeast-milk mixture, 1 egg, rested garlic, 2-3 Tbsp Boscoli olive salad (to taste), and ⅓ cup shredded three-cheddar blend. Stir until a thick, sticky dough forms. The dough will be wetter than traditional bread dough but hold together.
  7. 7
    Rise
    Transfer the dough to the prepared mini loaf pan. Smooth the top with a wet spatula or your fingers. Cover loosely with a clean kitchen towel. Let rise in a warm, draft-free spot for 45-60 minutes, until the dough reaches about ¾ the height of the pan. The rise time depends on room temperature.
  8. 8
    Bake
    Bake at 325°F for 40 minutes, or until the internal temperature reaches 210°F (99°C) and the top is golden brown. If using an instant-read thermometer, insert into the center of the loaf to check doneness.
  9. 9
    Cool before slicing
    Remove from oven and cool in the pan for 10 minutes, then turn out onto a cooling rack. Let rest for at least 15 more minutes before slicing. The crumb sets as it cools — slicing too early produces gummy texture.

🔬 Why each ingredient is here

  • Almond flour (replaces tapioca starch): provides superior protein (~6g per ounce), healthy monounsaturated fats, vitamin E (antioxidant), and magnesium (MTHFR methylation cofactor). Unlike empty-carb tapioca, almond flour slows glucose absorption and improves satiety. Trade-off: moderate oxalate — serve with calcium-rich foods if kidney stone-prone.
  • Sorghum + millet flour (GF base): both are Celiac-safe and low-oxalate. Sorghum provides resistant starch and polyphenols; millet adds B vitamins and a mild nutty flavor. Together they create a protein-rich matrix that slows glucose absorption compared to rice-based GF breads.
  • Ground flax seed (fresh): omega-3 ALA supports anti-inflammatory pathways (MTHFR, fatty liver, APOE4). Lignans provide phytoestrogen benefits. Soluble fiber slows gastric emptying and glucose release. Grinding fresh in a mortar and pestle preserves omega-3 integrity — pre-ground flax oxidizes quickly.
  • Boscoli Italian olive salad: delivers EVOO with oleocanthal (reduces amyloid-β aggregation for APOE4), oleic acid (improves hepatocyte insulin signaling for fatty liver / DP), and polyphenols (reduce NF-κB inflammation for MTHFR). The prepared salad includes olives, garlic, celery, and spices — all contribute additional phytonutrients without added sugar.
  • Three-cheddar cheese blend: provides complete protein, B12 (MTHFR methylation), and calcium that can help bind dietary oxalate from almond flour in the gut before absorption. Aged cheddar is naturally low-lactose (Celiac / dairy sensitivity).
  • Active dry yeast: creates a true bread rise and develops more complex flavor through fermentation. Yeast fermentation produces B vitamins and may improve mineral bioavailability. The rise time allows partial enzymatic breakdown of starches, potentially lowering glycemic response.
  • Garlic (3 cloves, crushed + 5 min rest): allicin activates AMPK (fatty liver / DP), induces glutathione synthesis (MTHFR), and supports healthy gut bacteria (Celiac). The 5-minute pre-rest is NOT optional — heat immediately deactivates alliinase before allicin can form. Therapeutic dose requires this step.
  • Psyllium husk (20g in base mix): soluble fiber forms a gel matrix in the dough, mimicking the gluten structure in wheat bread. Slows gastric emptying, glucose release, and feeds beneficial gut bacteria (Celiac / microbiome). Also supports regular bowel function and bile acid excretion (fatty liver).
  • Fresh rosemary + oregano: carnosic acid in rosemary is neuroprotective (APOE4) and hepatoprotective (fatty liver). Oregano provides carvacrol and rosmarinic acid — both reduce inflammation and support microbial balance. Use fresh rosemary for maximum carnosic acid content.
  • Black pepper (on garlic and throughout): piperine in black pepper enhances bioavailability of polyphenols from olive oil and herbs — pair with turmeric in cooking for the 2000% curcumin absorption boost.

⚠️ Oxalate Management — Important

This recipe has MODERATE oxalate due to the almond flour base (125g almond flour in the full base mix = approximately 40-45g per batch). While the cheddar cheese provides some calcium, it may not fully offset the oxalate load for individuals prone to kidney stones.

Recommendations:

  • Serve 1-2 slices per meal (not the entire loaf)
  • Pair with calcium-rich foods: Greek yogurt, kefir, additional cheese, or a glass of milk
  • Space high-oxalate meals throughout the day rather than concentrating them
  • Stay well-hydrated (2-3 liters water daily) to support kidney oxalate clearance
  • If you have a history of calcium-oxalate kidney stones, consider using this bread only occasionally, not daily

The original recipe using tapioca flour was LOW oxalate. This version prioritized nutritional density over oxalate minimization — a conscious trade-off for better protein, healthy fats, and elimination of empty carbs.
