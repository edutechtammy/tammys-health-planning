Low-Carb Velvet Egg Drop Soup (Starch-Free)

A savory, comforting Chinese restaurant classic modified for ultimate glycemic stability. Traditional cornstarch is replaced with finely ground flaxseed to achieve a rich, velvety texture without blood glucose spikes.

This version swaps starch for flax to keep the soup silky without a glycemic bump. The egg contribution gives a meaningful choline boost, while the flax brings a useful plant-based omega-3 signal. For APOE4 and fatty liver planning, the soup works as a light, nutrient-dense base that still supports blood sugar stability and satiety.

The texture key

Whisk flaxseed into cold broth first, then simmer briefly. This lets soluble fiber hydrate evenly and gives the soup a smooth body before the egg ribbons go in.
Ingredients
The Broth Base
  • 2 cups (16 oz)high-quality chicken bone broth
  • 1 tspgolden ground flaxseed, finely ground
  • 1/2 tsptoasted sesame oil
  • 1/4 tspground ginger (or 1/2 tsp freshly grated)
  • 1/4 tspsea salt, adjust to taste
  • 1/8 tspwhite pepper (or black pepper)
The Egg Ribbon
  • 2 largeeggs
  • 1 tspwater (for more delicate ribbons)
Optional Garnish
  • 1green onion, thinly sliced
Instructions
  1. 1
    Disperse the flaxseed
    In a small saucepan, pour in cold or room-temperature broth. Whisk in the flaxseed until fully dissolved with no clumps.
  2. 2
    Season and simmer
    Add sesame oil, ginger, sea salt, and white pepper. Bring to a gentle simmer over medium heat for 1-2 minutes until lightly thickened.
  3. 3
    Prep the egg
    Whisk eggs with 1 tsp water until yolks and whites are completely uniform and loose.
  4. 4
    Create the whirlpool
    Turn heat down to low so broth is barely bubbling. Stir in one continuous circular motion to create a steady whirlpool.
  5. 5
    Drop the egg
    Slowly drizzle whisked egg into the outer edge of the moving whirlpool in a thin, steady stream. Do not dump all at once.
  6. 6
    Set and serve
    Remove from heat immediately and let sit undisturbed for 60 seconds so ribbons finish cooking through.
  7. 7
    Garnish
    Pour into a bowl, top with sliced green onion, and serve hot.
