Overnight Sous Vide Mediterranean Pinto Beans (12 oz Jar)

Pre-soaked pinto beans cooked overnight with Mediterranean herbs and extra virgin olive oil. The gentle sous vide cooking creates perfectly tender beans that mash into creamy, oil-shielded refried beans while retaining their mineral-rich cooking liquid.

⏱ 10 min prep + 8 hours cook (+ 8-12 hrs soak) 🍽 1.5 cup jar ❄️ Keeps 5 days refrigerated
DP / Blood Sugar Celiac / GF
🥗 Ingredients & Method
🧾 Ingredients — 1.5 cup jar
  • 0.33 cup dry pinto beans (soaked 8-12 hours and drained)
  • 0.66 cup water
  • 1 pinch dried oregano
  • 1 small garlic clove (smashed)
  • 1 tsp extra virgin olive oil
🔪 Method — 8 hours total
  1. 1
    Combine ingredients
    Combine pre-soaked pinto beans, water, oregano, smashed garlic, and extra virgin olive oil in the 12 oz mason jar.
  2. 2
    Seal jar
    Ensure a proper headroom buffer remains at the top, wipe the rim, and secure the lid fingertip-tight.
  3. 3
    Place in water bath
    Arrange the jar in the multi-cooker pot, leaving the center open. Fill the pot with warm water up to the jar neck.
  4. 4
    Cook sous vide
    Run the Sous Vide preset at 180°F (82.2°C) for 8 hours overnight.
  5. 5
    Mash and serve
    In the morning, unseal the jar and mash the tender beans directly in their mineral-rich cooking liquid to create creamy, oil-shielded refried beans.

🩸 Low glycemic — Pinto beans provide resistant starch and fiber, olive oil further blunts glucose response
