Hearty Beef Vegetable Soup

A meal-prep soup using one full pound of ground beef plus stewed tomatoes, potatoes, peas, onion, and 907 g of bone broth. The nutrient section is set to a 300 g serving size so the recipe and menu planner stay aligned.

Ingredients
  • 1 lb (454 g)ground beef
  • 2 cansstewed tomatoes (about 14.5 oz / 411 g each, with juices)
  • 2 largepotatoes, peeled and diced
  • 1 canyoung green peas, drained
  • 1 largeonion, diced
  • 907 gbone broth
  • 1 tspsea salt, plus more to taste
  • 1/2 tspblack pepper
  • Optional1 tsp dried oregano and 1 bay leaf
Instructions
  1. 1
    Brown base
    Cook ground beef and diced onion in a large pot over medium heat for 8 to 10 minutes until beef is browned and onion is soft. Drain excess fat if needed.
  2. 2
    Build soup
    Add stewed tomatoes with juices, diced potatoes, drained peas, and 907 g bone broth. Season with salt, pepper, oregano, and bay leaf if using.
  3. 3
    Simmer
    Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Cook 30 to 35 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the potatoes are tender.
  4. 4
    Portion
    Remove bay leaf, adjust seasoning, and weigh portions at 300 g each for consistent tracking and menu planning.
