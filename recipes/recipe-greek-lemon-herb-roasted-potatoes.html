Greek Lemon Herb Roasted Potatoes (Patates Lemonates)

These lemony roasted Yukon Gold potatoes are crisp at the edges, tender inside, and full of classic Greek flavor from extra virgin olive oil, garlic, oregano, and fresh lemon. A quick par-boil helps release surface starch, shorten roast time, and make the crust crispier without adding much effort. They work beautifully as a side dish when eaten last in a protein-first meal order.

⏱ Prep: 10 min 🔥 Cook: 40–50 min ⏳ Total: ~60 min 🍽 Servings: 4

🧾 Ingredients — 4 servings
  • 1.5 lbYukon Gold potatoes🟢 starch
  • 1/4 cupExtra virgin olive oil🟢 EVOO
  • 1 largeLemon, juiced🟢 bright
  • 1/2 cupLow-sodium broth⬜ zero
  • 4 clovesGarlic, minced⬜ zero
  • 1.5 tbspDried oregano⬜ trace
  • 1 tspOnion powder⬜ trace
  • 1/2 tspSea salt⬜ zero
  • 1/4 tspBlack pepper🟢 low
  • to tasteFresh parsley or lemon wedges🟢 garnish
👩‍🍳 Method
  1. 1
    Par-boil the wedges
    Place the potato wedges in a pot of salted cold water. Bring to a boil and cook for 5 minutes, then drain thoroughly. This quick steam step reduces surface starch and helps the potatoes roast with crisper edges.
  2. 2
    Whisk the lemon-herb mixture
    In a bowl, whisk together the olive oil, lemon juice, broth, garlic, oregano, onion powder, sea salt, and black pepper until well combined.
  3. 3
    Coat and arrange
    Add the drained potatoes to a 9x13-inch baking dish. Pour the lemon-herb mixture over the potatoes and toss until coated. Spread them into a single layer to encourage even browning.
  4. 4
    Roast
    Roast uncovered at 400°F (200°C) for 40 to 50 minutes, flipping the potatoes halfway through. They are done when most of the liquid is absorbed, the potatoes are fork-tender, and the edges are golden and crisp.
  5. 5
    Optional resistant starch step
    If you have time, let the cooked potatoes cool and refrigerate them overnight. Reheat gently before serving to convert part of the digestible starch to resistant starch, which may lower the total blood sugar impact.

Glycemic & Blood Sugar Control Notes

  • Portion target: Keep this to roughly 1/2 cup to 3/4 cup per serving to keep the total carbohydrate load moderate.
  • Meal order strategy: Eat a non-starchy green salad first, then your protein, and finish with the lemon potatoes at the end of the meal to flatten the glucose curve.
  • Timing: This is best served for dinner or later in the day, when insulin sensitivity is generally more favorable and any morning Dawn phenomenon spikes are less likely to be compounded.
⚠️ Potato strategy: Potatoes are not a “free” starch, even when roasted well. The goal here is not to make them “safe,” but to use the best practical strategy: moderate portion, protein-first meal order, later timing, and optional resistant starch conversion if you want the lowest blood sugar impact.

Keep the portion modest and pair it with a greens + protein first. That makes this a much more blood-sugar-friendly side than serving the potatoes on their own.
