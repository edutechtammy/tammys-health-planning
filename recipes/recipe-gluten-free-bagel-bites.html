Gluten-Free Bagel Bites

This recipe came from insulinresistant1's YouTube channel and fits your condition stack as a strong Type 2 diabetes / blood sugar candidate. The dough is just almond flour, baking powder, and Greek yogurt (your Sugar Shift yogurt works), so it stays low in net carbs per bite while giving better protein than a typical flour-based snack. Because almond flour is naturally gluten-free, it also fits the Celiac-safe side of your stack.

Prep: 10 min Cook: 15-20 min Yield: about 8 bites Oven: 400°F Net carbs: ~2.0g per bite
Celiac / GF DP / Type 2 Blood Sugar Fatty Liver Kidney Stone (portion-aware)

Condition note: low net carbs + moderate protein make this a practical test item for Type 2 glucose stability. The almond flour base is moderate oxalate, so portion size still matters for kidney stone prevention.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup almond flour
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt (Sugar Shift yogurt in your version)
  • 2 tablespoons everything bagel seasoning (salted seed mix), or enough to coat
If your everything-bagel mix includes sesame and poppy seeds, keep portion size moderate for kidney stone prevention because both are higher-oxalate seed toppings.

Method

  1. Preheat oven to 400°F (205°C). Line a baking tray with parchment.
  2. Mix almond flour and baking powder in a bowl.
  3. Add Greek yogurt and mix until a soft, cohesive dough forms.
  4. Divide dough into 8 equal pieces and roll each into a ball.
  5. Roll each ball in everything-bagel seasoning so the outside is well coated.
  6. Bake for 15 to 20 minutes, until set and lightly golden.
  7. Cool briefly and test first with a measured serving if you are validating glucose response.

Tracking Notes

  • Source: insulinresistant1 YouTube channel.
  • For Type 2 testing, run this as a stand-alone snack first before combining with other carbs.
  • If using a salty seasoning blend, pair with hydration and avoid adding extra salt on top.

If testing blood sugar response, log baseline and T+60/T+120 with a fixed number of bites.
