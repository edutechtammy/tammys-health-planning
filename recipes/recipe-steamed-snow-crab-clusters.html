Steamed Snow Crab Clusters with Garlic-Lemon EVOO & Herbs

Snow crab clusters steamed briefly and served with warm garlic, lemon, and herb-infused EVOO. This is a clean, zero-spike seafood option built around the APOE4/FTO pattern: low saturated fat, easy protein, very low glycemic load, and a strong choline + marine omega-3 contribution that supports the liver and the brain.

⏱ 10 min prep + overnight thaw 🔥 5–7 min steaming 🍽 2 generous portions 🌿 GF / grain-free

Ingredients
  • 1.5 lbfrozen cooked snow crab clusters, thawed overnight in the refrigerator
  • 3 tbspcold-pressed extra virgin olive oil
  • 2 clovesfresh garlic, crushed and rested 5 minutes
  • 1 tbspfresh lemon juice, plus extra wedges for serving
  • 1 tbspfresh flat-leaf parsley, finely chopped
  • pinchsea salt and freshly cracked black pepper
Instructions
  1. 1
    Thaw
    Place the frozen crab clusters on a rimmed plate or tray in the refrigerator overnight to thaw completely.
  2. 2
    Warm the dip
    Combine the EVOO, activated garlic, lemon juice, parsley, salt, and pepper in a small saucepan or dip bowl. Warm gently over very low heat for 2–3 minutes until fragrant, but do not boil the oil.
  3. 3
    Steam
    Add 1 to 2 inches of water to a large pot fitted with a steamer basket and bring to a rolling boil. Place the thawed crab clusters in the basket, cover, and steam for 5–7 minutes until fully heated and sweet-smelling.
  4. 4
    Serve
    Remove the crab with tongs and serve immediately with the warm garlic-lemon EVOO dip, extra lemon wedges, and a side of massaged kale or roasted zucchini if you want extra volume.
