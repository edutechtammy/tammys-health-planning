Sugar Shift Pumpkin Spice Mousse

Don't let the word mousse fool you. This is a functional dessert built from strained Sugar Shift yogurt, pure pumpkin, chia, hemp hearts, psyllium, and warm autumn spices. The live cultures stay active while the viscous fiber system hydrates into a dense gel that slows glucose response and makes the bowl feel far richer than its sugar load suggests. It is gluten-free, grain-free, low-oxalate, and designed to behave like a metabolic shield instead of a spike.

🧾 Ingredients — 1 large serving
  • 155gHomemade Sugar Shift Yogurt, strained thick⬜ live culture base
  • 100gLibby's 100% Pure Canned Pumpkin⬜ low ox
  • 15gWhole Chia Seeds⬜ gel network
  • 10gHemp Hearts⬜ fat texture
  • 3gWhole Psyllium Husk⬜ viscous fiber
  • 15gLakanto Monkfruit Sweetener with Allulose⬜ sweetener
  • 2gCeylon Cinnamon⬜ spice
  • pinchGround Ginger⬜ spice
  • pinchGround Nutmeg⬜ spice
  • dashVanilla Extract⬜ aroma
  • optionalPre-salted Pepitas or Extra Cinnamon⬜ garnish
👩‍🍳 Instructions
  1. 1
    Build the base
    Combine the strained Sugar Shift yogurt and pumpkin in a medium bowl. Whisk vigorously until the color is completely uniform and the mixture is smooth.
  2. 2
    Add the autumn spices
    Add the monkfruit-allulose sweetener, Ceylon cinnamon, ginger, nutmeg, and vanilla. Whisk again until the sweetener disappears into the pumpkin matrix.
  3. 3
    Introduce the gel network
    Sprinkle the chia seeds, hemp hearts, and psyllium husk over the mixture. Fold with a spatula until everything is evenly distributed.
  4. 4
    Hydrate and chill
    Cover and refrigerate for 10 to 15 minutes. The mousse should thicken noticeably as the chia and psyllium hydrate.
  5. 5
    Garnish and serve
    Finish with extra cinnamon or a few salted pepitas if you want a little crunch. Serve cold.

Why this works

  • Chia plus psyllium creates the viscous soluble fiber system that slows the glucose curve.
  • Pumpkin gives the autumn flavour without pushing the net carbs very high.
  • The strained Sugar Shift yogurt keeps the bowl cultured, tangy, and choline-supportive.
  • Chill time is not optional if you want the mousse texture to fully set.

For CGM testing, log it as a dessert or evening snack and note whether the 10-15 minute chill changes the response compared with eating it immediately.
