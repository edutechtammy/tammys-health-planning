Herby Lentil, White Bean & Cabbage Crunch Salad

A legume-heavy salad with green lentils, white beans, crunchy cabbage, cucumber, and a bright lemon-herb dressing. It is built to be protein-dense, fiber-rich, and very low in saturated fat while still tasting fresh and strong.

⏱ Prep: 12 min 🍽 Yield: 1 large serving 🥗 Smart Protein
APOE4 Celiac / GF DP / Blood Sugar Fatty Liver MTHFR
Low GI • dense legume structure

Smart Protein check: 19.2g protein, 0.6g saturated fat, and a 32:1 protein-to-sat-fat ratio.

Ingredients
  • 1/2 cupgreen lentils, cooked and cooled
  • 1/2 cupcannellini beans
  • 1.5 cupsshredded cabbage
  • 1/2 cupcucumber, diced
  • 1 tbspparsley, chopped
  • 1 tbspdill, chopped
  • 1 tbspmint, chopped
  • 1.5 tbsplemon juice
  • 1.5 tspEVOO
  • 1/2 tspDijon mustard
  • to tastegarlic powder, oregano, salt, pepper
Method
  1. 1
    Whisk the dressing
    Mix the lemon juice, EVOO, Dijon, garlic powder, oregano, salt, and pepper until emulsified.
  2. 2
    Build the base
    Fold in the lentils, cannellini beans, cabbage, cucumber, parsley, dill, and mint.
  3. 3
    Dress and toss
    Pour the dressing over the salad and toss until everything is coated and crunchy.
  4. 4
    Serve
    Enjoy immediately for a very satisfying, slow-digesting lunch bowl.

Why it fits

  • Green lentils and beans together give you both protein and viscous fiber in a stable, low-saturated-fat profile.
  • The brassica crunch and herbs make the salad feel substantial without needing extra starch or fatty toppings.
  • It is a strong repeat lunch option when you want a lot of fullness and very low saturated fat.
