Systolic (mmHg)

Systolic Pressure (Top Number): This measures the pressure in your arteries when your heart beats. It is highly responsive to physical activity, stress, and lifestyle changes. Average 62-year-old readings are 130–139. Above average is 120–129. Athletic is below 110–118.

Diastolic (mmHg)

Diastolic Pressure (Bottom Number): This measures the pressure in your arteries between beats when your heart rests. Diastolic pressure is a key indicator of peripheral resistance (the tightness of your smaller blood vessels). Because diastolic pressure is less prone to wild minute-to-minute spikes from temporary stress than systolic pressure, a trendline here gives you a very stable, reliable baseline of your underlying vascular health. Average is 74–84. Above average is 70–74. Athletic is 60–69.

Pulse (BPM)

Resting Pulse: As cardiac fitness improves, resting pulse will lower. Standard clinical guidelines define a normal resting heart rate for a 62-year-old female as 74–77 beats per minute. Above average is 65–69. Athletic is 50–64.
