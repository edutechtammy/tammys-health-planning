Rice Paper Potstickers

Rice paper folded around a shrimp, ginger, garlic, and napa cabbage filling — pan-fried until the flat bottom is deeply golden and crispy, then finished with a water splash under a lid to steam the top through. The pan-fry/steam sequence is identical to classic wheat-dumpling potsticker technique; the rice paper performs the same way with no wheat required. The result is crispy on the bottom, tender on the folded top — the defining potsticker texture. Shrimp is ideal here: high protein, fast-cooking, very low oxalate, APOE4-compatible. Ready in about 20 minutes from start to plate.

⏱ 10-second soak for potstickers — shorter than any other format

The shorter soak time is intentional. You need enough pliability to fold the half-moon without cracking, but if the sheet is fully soft it won't hold the crimp under heat. The sheet finishes softening from the steam step — you don't need it soft to begin with. This is the single most common mistake: over-soaking produces a fold that opens during frying.

✅ Do not move them during the first fry stage

Rice paper sticks to the pan initially — this is expected. It will release cleanly on its own once the base has crisped and browned. If you try to move a potsticker early you will tear the bottom off. Set a timer for 3 minutes and leave them completely alone.

⚠️ The water splash — stand back

When you add water to a hot pan with oil, it will spit aggressively. Use a cup measure to pour it in from a slight distance, and get the lid on fast. The steaming phase is what cooks the filling through — without it the shrimp near the top of each potsticker may be undercooked even when the base looks done.

🥟 Method
  1. 1
    Mix filling — raw shrimp
    Combine all filling ingredients. Do not pre-cook — shrimp cooks during frying and steaming. Chop shrimp roughly (not minced) — some texture is better than a paste.
  2. 2
    Soak one sheet — 10 seconds only
    Barely pliable. Work one sheet at a time — do not pre-soak multiple sheets.
  3. 3
    Fill, fold, crimp
    Place 1.5 tbsp filling on the lower half, leaving a 2cm border. Fold the top half over. Press the edge firmly. Fold the seam over itself once. Repeat ×16.
  4. 4
    Pan-fry flat side down — 3–4 min
    Heat oil in non-stick or cast iron over medium-high. Place potstickers flat side down, batch of 8. Cook undisturbed 3–4 min until the base is deeply golden. Do not move early.
  5. 5
    Add ¼ cup water, lid on immediately
    Pour water in, cover fast — it spits. Steam 3–4 min until water fully evaporates.
  6. 6
    Lid off — re-crisp 30–60 sec
    Remove lid, let base re-crisp briefly. Remove and serve immediately with dipping sauce.
🔄 Filling variations
  • Ground turkey + mushroom: Replace shrimp with 250g ground turkey + 100g finely chopped mushrooms (cremini or shiitake). Deeper umami. Increase cook time by 2 minutes as turkey takes longer to cook through than shrimp.
  • Chicken + coriander + lime: Ground chicken + ginger + garlic + fresh coriander leaf + lime zest + coconut aminos. A brighter flavour profile than the classic version — more Thai-leaning.
  • Vegetarian — firm tofu + mushroom + cabbage: Press tofu very dry (30 min in a clean cloth under weight). Crumble finely. Finely chopped shiitake + napa cabbage + ginger + garlic + coconut aminos. The key is getting the tofu dry enough — wet tofu produces a filling that steams poorly and breaks the rice paper.
  • Triangle fold: Instead of a half-moon, fold the sheet into a triangle — bring one corner to the opposite edge. Produces a more compact, three-layered shape with a thicker seal and more chew in the fold.
  • Air fryer version (no stovetop): Skip the pan-fry/steam entirely. Brush both sides with avocado oil. Air fry at 380°F for 10–12 minutes, flipping once at 6 minutes. Produces an all-over crisp exterior without the defined crispy bottom/soft top contrast of the stovetop method. Different texture — equally good, but not quite "potsticker."

🩸 Very low GI — shrimp is essentially zero carbohydrate. Rice paper wrapper contributes ~7g carb per potsticker at GL ~5 each. Suitable at any time of day.
