📅 Week 3 Tasting Set

Fresh Herb EVOO Dressings

Four dressings using fresh herbs — the most vibrant of the five sets but the most time-sensitive. Fresh herbs deliver higher folate and more volatile flavour compounds than their dried equivalents, but they also introduce perishability. All four are short shelf life. Use orange or yellow labels on these bottles to distinguish them immediately from the green-label standard-life bottles in the same drawer.

⚠️ Make 6 oz batches only — not 12 oz. 6 oz = 6 × 1-oz bottles = 3 days' supply for 2 people eating daily salads. This matches the safe window. Formula: 9 tbsp EVOO + 3 tbsp acid = 12 tbsp = 6 fl oz. Divides to: 3 oz (3 bottles · 1.5-day supply) if needed.
⚠️ Two shelf life windows in this set — know which is which:
Recipes 8 & 9 (no raw garlic): 3–5 days refrigerated — use an orange/yellow label.
Recipes 10 & 11 (contain fresh crushed garlic): 3 days maximum — hard limit. Fresh garlic submerged in oil creates anaerobic conditions that allow Clostridium botulinum spores to activate and produce botulinum toxin without any visible spoilage. You cannot smell or see the hazard — the 3-day rule is enforced by the clock, not your nose. Use a red label on these two bottles and write the batch date on the label. Discard on day 4 even if they smell and look fine.
Bottle label guide — this set
🟡 Orange label — Recipes 8 & 9 · 3–5 days refrigerated (no raw garlic)
🔴 Red label — Recipes 10 & 11 · 3-day hard limit (fresh garlic in oil) · write batch date on label

All four dressings are very low GI at a 1-oz serving. No food-response testing needed for the dressing alone.

8 Fresh Dill & Lemon Dressing
🟡 3–5 days · orange label · 6 oz batch
Ingredients — 6 oz batch
    Oil
  • 9 tbspExtra-virgin olive oil (= ½ cup + 1 tbsp)
    • Acid
  • 2 tbspFresh-squeezed lemon juice
  • 1 tbspWater
    • Fresh herb
  • 2 tbspFresh dill, finely chopped
    • Emulsifier & seasoning
  • 1 tspDijon mustard
  • ¼ tspFine sea salt
  • pinchBlack pepper
9 + 2 + 1 = 12 tbsp = 6 fl oz ✓ · Makes 6 bottles · 3-day supply for 2 people
Method
  1. 1
    Chop dill finely
    The dill needs to be fine enough to pass through the silicone bottle tip without clogging. Finely chop with a sharp knife — small flecks, not long strands.
  2. 2
    Combine & shake
    Add all ingredients to jar. Seal and shake 30 seconds.
  3. 3
    Fill & label
    Fill 6 × 1-oz silicone bottles. Orange/yellow label. Write batch date on label. Refrigerate. Use within 3–5 days.
What's working: Fresh dill preserves folate that dried dill loses in the drying process — directly relevant for MTHFR. Lemon juice delivers limonene and urinary citrate (kidney stone protection). Dijon emulsifies for a more stable suspension than the dried-herb dressings. The flavor is tzatziki-adjacent — cool, bright, herbal. Best on: cucumber-tomato salads, cold poached fish, alongside grilled salmon, with the feta-and-olive salads from the Mediterranean recipe cluster. Oxalate: low.
9 Parsley & Red Wine Vinegar Dressing
🟡 3–5 days · orange label · 6 oz batch
Ingredients — 6 oz batch
    Oil
  • 9 tbspExtra-virgin olive oil (= ½ cup + 1 tbsp)
    • Acid
  • 2 tbspRed wine vinegar
  • 1 tbspFresh-squeezed lemon juice
    • Fresh herb
  • 2 tbspFresh flat-leaf parsley, very finely chopped
    • Seasoning
  • ¼ tspFine sea salt
  • pinchBlack pepper
  • optional½ tsp garlic powder
9 + 2 + 1 = 12 tbsp = 6 fl oz ✓ · Use flat-leaf (Italian) parsley for stronger flavour; curly parsley works but is milder
Method
  1. 1
    Chop parsley very fine
    Flat-leaf parsley chopped finely enough to pass through the bottle tip without clogging. Remove thick stems — use only the leaf and thin stem portions.
  2. 2
    Combine & shake
    Add all ingredients to jar. No emulsifier in this recipe — it separates quickly. Shake 20 seconds to combine. Shake each bottle firmly before squeezing every time.
  3. 3
    Fill & label
    Fill 6 × 1-oz silicone bottles. Orange/yellow label. Write batch date. Refrigerate. Use within 3–5 days.
What's working: Fresh flat-leaf parsley is a meaningful source of raw folate (MTHFR relevant), vitamin K, and apigenin (anti-inflammatory flavonoid). Red wine vinegar adds resveratrol and anthocyanin residues. The flavor is clean, light, and slightly grassy — the least assertive of the fresh herb set, which makes it the most versatile. Best on: delicate mixed greens, avocado salads, tomato-mozzarella (or tomato-feta), as a light drizzle over roasted vegetables. Oxalate: parsley is moderate-oxalate; at 2 tbsp per 6-oz batch, each 1-oz serving contains a small but noted amount — consume alongside calcium food if eating multiple servings.
10 Basil-Lemon-Garlic Dressing
🔴 3-day hard limit · red label · write batch date
Ingredients — 6 oz batch
⚠️ Contains fresh garlic in oil. 3-day maximum regardless of smell or appearance. Write batch date on the red label. Discard on day 4.
    Oil
  • 9 tbspExtra-virgin olive oil (= ½ cup + 1 tbsp)
    • Acid
  • 2 tbspFresh-squeezed lemon juice
  • 1 tbspRed wine vinegar
    • Aromatics — activate garlic first
  • 1 cloveGarlic, crushed — rest 5 min before adding
  • 2 tbspFresh basil, very finely chopped
    • Seasoning
  • ¼ tspFine sea salt
  • pinchBlack pepper
9 + 2 + 1 = 12 tbsp = 6 fl oz ✓ · ⚠️ Clock starts when garlic goes into the oil — label with today's date
Method
  1. 1
    Activate garlic first
    Crush the garlic clove and set aside for 5 minutes. The 5-minute rest completes the alliinase-to-allicin conversion before oil coats the surface. This is the only window — once garlic is submerged in oil, the enzyme is blocked.
  2. 2
    Chop basil
    Finely chop fresh basil leaves — fine enough to pass through the bottle tip.
  3. 3
    Combine & shake
    Add all ingredients to jar. Seal and shake 30 seconds.
  4. 4
    Fill, label with date & refrigerate
    Fill 6 × 1-oz silicone bottles. Red label — write today's date visibly. Refrigerate. Discard after 3 days, no exceptions.
What's working: Fresh basil — linalool, eugenol, and rosmarinic acid (anti-inflammatory; highest in fresh, not dried). The 5-min garlic activation delivers allicin in the finished dressing — a raw application, so the full dose is intact. Lemon limonene and citrate. The flavour is a pesto-dressing hybrid — more complex than the others. Best on: caprese-style salads (tomato + feta), pasta salads (GF pasta), roasted red pepper salads, alongside ladenia. Oxalate: low — basil at 2 tbsp per batch is a minor contributor per 1-oz serving.
11 Greek Taverna Dressing
🔴 3-day hard limit · red label · write batch date
Ingredients — 6 oz batch
⚠️ Contains fresh garlic in oil. 3-day maximum regardless of smell or appearance. Write batch date on the red label.
    Oil
  • 9 tbspExtra-virgin olive oil (= ½ cup + 1 tbsp)
    • Acid
  • 3 tbspRed wine vinegar
    • Aromatics — activate garlic first
  • 1 cloveGarlic, crushed — rest 5 min before adding
  • 1 tbspFresh oregano leaves, finely chopped (or 1 tsp dried if fresh unavailable)
    • Emulsifier & seasoning
  • ½ tspDijon mustard
  • ¼ tspFine sea salt
  • pinchBlack pepper
9 + 3 = 12 tbsp = 6 fl oz ✓ · This is the boldest, most assertive dressing in the whole set of 16 — a little goes a long way. ⚠️ Label with today's date.
Method
  1. 1
    Activate garlic
    Crush garlic, set aside exactly 5 minutes. Start prepping the oregano while waiting.
  2. 2
    Chop oregano
    Finely chop fresh oregano leaves. Fresh oregano is significantly more potent than dried — 1 tbsp fresh is roughly equivalent to 1 tsp dried. If fresh is unavailable, substitute 1 tsp dried oregano (no shelf-life concern with the dried version, but then this becomes a 3–5 day dressing rather than a 3-day one — still label with date either way since the garlic governs).
  3. 3
    Combine & shake
    Add all ingredients including the rested crushed garlic to jar. Seal and shake vigorously 30 seconds.
  4. 4
    Fill, date label & refrigerate
    Fill 6 × 1-oz bottles. Red label with today's date visible. Refrigerate. Discard after 3 days.
What's working: This is the closest to a traditional Greek taverna dressing — raw activated garlic delivering maximum allicin (AMPK activation, hepatoprotective, glutathione induction for MTHFR), fresh oregano delivering rosmarinic acid at its highest concentration, red wine vinegar with resveratrol, raw EVOO with oleocanthal fully preserved. The Dijon adds just enough body without changing the profile. Best on: classic Greek salad (cucumber, tomato, olives, feta), any hearty romaine or mixed greens, alongside ladenia and tzatziki as a full mezze. This is the one that will taste most like "actual Greek restaurant dressing." Oxalate: very low.
