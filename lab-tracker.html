🩺 Labs to Request & Track (click to collapse)

Metabolic & Pre-diabetes

  • HbA1c does not work for me due to the RBC longevity / HbA1c masking cluster — ANK1, HK1, TMPRSS6 genetics. Instead, I need to request the frucosimine or glycated albumin test.
  • Fasting insulin + glucose → HOMA-IR (insulin resistance score)
  • Fasting triglycerides + HDL (metabolic syndrome markers)
  • Uric acid — elevated in stone and pre-diabetes

Methylation

  • Homocysteine — target <8 µmol/L (ideally <7)
  • RBC folate (not serum) — better marker of tissue stores
  • Serum B12 + MMA (methylmalonic acid) if B12 is borderline. In my own labs I ran in the 400s without methylated folate and B12. This is on the lower side of what is normal. With supplementation of those, I have been running in the 600s. Keep an eye on it with occasional testing over time.

Celiac Recovery

  • tTG-IgA — monitors gut healing (should normalise 12–18 mo strict GF)
  • Ferritin (not just hemoglobin)
  • 25-OH Vitamin D
  • Zinc, RBC magnesium

Kidney Stone

  • 24-hour urine collection (gold standard): oxalate, citrate, calcium, uric acid, volume, pH
  • Serum calcium + PTH — rule out hyperparathyroidism
  • Urinalysis with microscopy — monitor for crystals / infection

Fatty Liver / Hepatic Health

  • ALT (Alanine Aminotransferase) — primary hepatocyte damage marker; trends down as liver fat clears
  • AST (Aspartate Aminotransferase) — secondary liver marker; AST/ALT ratio >1 suggests advancing disease
  • GGT (Gamma-glutamyl transferase) — most sensitive early stress marker; also tracks hepatic glutathione status and oxidative load
  • ALP (Alkaline Phosphatase) — general liver/bile duct health
  • Liver ultrasound — qualitative finding every 6–12 months; echogenicity tracks fat reduction progress
  • Fasting triglycerides + HOMA-IR — already in Metabolic panel; also the primary hepatic fat export and IR markers

Klebsiella / Gut

  • Comprehensive stool panel (GI Effects, Doctor's Data, or similar)
  • Urine culture if any UTI symptoms — Klebsiella requires targeted antibiotics
  • Consider urine oxalate in 24-hour panel to assess Oxalobacter gap

APOE4 / Cognitive

  • Lipid panel with LDL particle size (NMR preferred for APOE4)
  • hsCRP (high-sensitivity C-reactive protein) — inflammation marker
  • APOE genotyping (if not already done — confirms e3/e4 vs e4/e4)
  • Baseline cognitive screening (MoCA) — establishes baseline now at 62
  • ⭐ pTau217 / β-Amyloid 1-42 Plasma Ratio (Lumipulse G) — research study, blood draw May 19 — detects amyloid plaque accumulation via blood draw (~95% concordant with PET scan); critical early marker for APOE4 carriers
  • ⭐ pTau blood test (pg/mL) — result logged May 26, 2026: 0.10 pg/mL ✅ reassuring — measures phosphorylated tau protein (tau tangle formation marker); independent of amyloid; low result alongside negative amyloid ratio is doubly reassuring for APOE4; retest every 2–3 years for longitudinal baseline
