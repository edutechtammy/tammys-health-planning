Metabolic Milestone Bands
  • Liver & Pancreas Unloading: 44-46 in waist (early visceral fat unloading, often within first 5-10% loss).
  • Sub-0.60 WHtR Threshold: 40-41 in waist (crosses below 0.60 WHtR, reduced inflammatory pressure).
  • Reversed Metabolic Risk Zone: 34-35 in waist (approaches/enters WHtR < 0.50 territory).
  • Optimal Metabolic Goal Band: at or under 34 in waist (aligned with your 160 lb target phase).
