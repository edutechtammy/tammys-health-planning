☐ GlycineGlycine 3 g Evening With or without food MTHFR - methylation -
PRIMARY METHYL BUFFER — actively clears excess methyl groups when overmethylated. Homocysteine sits at a crossroads. MTHFR reduces PEMT (phosphatidylethanolamine N-methyltransferase) pathway activity — the route by which the body synthesises choline endogenously — meaning dietary choline cannot always be compensated internally when methylation is impaired. Alpha GPC is the most bioavailable choline form and crosses the blood-brain barrier, where it supports acetylcholine signalling — directly relevant to APOE4's elevated cognitive risk profile. Why 150 mg and not more: ⚠️ High choline intake is converted by gut bacteria to TMA (trimethylamine), which the liver oxidises to TMAO (trimethylamine N-oxide) — a pro-atherogenic compound that promotes vascular plaque formation and arterial inflammation. APOE4 carriers already carry elevated cardiovascular risk, making high TMAO specifically more dangerous for this genotype than for the general population. Supplemental choline raises TMAO more than food-source choline at equivalent doses. 150 mg is a conservative safety-net dose that provides meaningful choline support without triggering the TMAO risk seen at higher supplemental doses (300–600 mg, such as in standard Alpha GPC products). Now that sunflower lecithin is in the daily protocol, this is rarely needed.
