Olive & Feta Cheese Bread (GF Sourdough-Style)

A hearty gluten-free loaf built on a millet-sorghum base with generous EVOO, kalamata olives, feta, and garlic. Kefir ferments the batter slightly during mixing, contributing a sourdough tang and lowering the glycaemic response compared to an unfermented GF bread. Feta provides the calcium needed to bind any trace oxalate from the olives in the gut. Works as the bread component on any meal day — and doubles as the food-test sourdough vehicle.

🧾 Ingredients — 8 slices

  • ½ cupSorghum flour🟢 low ox
  • ¼ cupTapioca starch⬜ zero
  • 1 tspPsyllium husk powder🟢 low ox
  • 1 tspGF baking powder⬜ zero
  • ½ tspFine sea salt⬜ zero
  • 3 largeEggs⬜ zero
  • ⅓ cupEVOO + extra for pan⬜ zero
  • ½ cupPlain homemade kefir (or full-fat yogurt)⬜ zero
  • ½ cupKalamata olives, pitted & roughly chopped🟢 low ox
  • ½ cupFeta cheese, crumbled🟢 + Ca²⁺
  • 3 clovesGarlic, crushed (rest 5 min)🟢 low ox
  • 1 tspFresh rosemary, finely chopped🟢 low ox
  • ½ tspDried oregano🟢 low ox
  • to tasteFreshly ground black pepper🟢 low ox
Overall oxalate: Low. Feta calcium covers any trace oxalate from olives and herbs — no additional calcium pairing needed as long as feta is included. Calcium is equally covered when using mozzarella as a substitute (see swap note below).
🧀 Lower-sodium cheese swap
Standard feta carries ~830mg sodium per ½ cup (75g). Two options:

Half-and-half (recommended): ~40g homemade part-skim mozzarella squeezed dry + ~35g feta. Cuts sodium by ~50%, reduces sat fat, preserves flavour. Add ¼ tsp lemon zest to the batter. Calcium coverage maintained.

Full mozzarella (kefir-milk batch): Use 75g kefir-milk mozzarella — made with 20–25% plain kefir replacing part of the milk (see the Homemade Mozzarella recipe for exact method). Squeeze very dry before folding in — fresh mozzarella releases moisture in a batter loaf if not pressed. Add 1–2 tsp nutritional yeast + ¼ tsp lemon zest to replace feta's sharpness. Sodium drops ~90%. The kefir mozzarella's lactic tang in the olive-garlic-rosemary environment is a genuine functional substitute in this loaf specifically.
👩‍🍳 Instructions
  1. 1
    Activate the garlic
    Crush or finely chop garlic and set aside for 5 minutes before using. This allows alliinase to convert alliin into allicin — the active therapeutic compound. Don't skip or shorten this step.
  2. 2
    Prep the pan
    Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Generously grease a 9×5-inch loaf pan with EVOO. Line the bottom with a strip of parchment for easy release.
  3. 3
    Mix dry ingredients
    Whisk millet flour, sorghum flour, tapioca starch, psyllium husk, baking powder, salt, oregano, rosemary, and black pepper together in a large bowl.
  4. 4
    Mix wet ingredients
    In a separate bowl whisk eggs until lightly beaten. Add EVOO, kefir, and the rested garlic. Whisk to combine.
  5. 5
    Combine & fold
    Pour wet into dry and stir until a thick batter forms. Fold in chopped olives and crumbled feta gently — distribute without breaking them down.
  6. 6
    Bake
    Pour into the prepared pan, smooth the top, and drizzle a ribbon of EVOO over the surface. Bake 40–42 minutes until golden on top and a toothpick comes out clean from the centre.
  7. 7
    Cool before slicing
    Rest in the pan for 15 minutes before turning out. Slice into 8 pieces. The crumb firms up as it cools — cleaner slices after a full rest.

🔬 Why each ingredient is here

  • Millet + sorghum flour (GF base): both are Celiac-safe, low-oxalate, and provide a denser protein matrix than rice flour — slowing glucose absorption compared to a rice-based GF bread.
  • EVOO (⅓ cup + surface drizzle): oleocanthal reduces amyloid-β aggregation (APOE4); oleic acid improves hepatocyte insulin signalling (fatty liver / DP); polyphenols reduce NF-κB inflammation (MTHFR). Use generously — the fat content is intentional and lowers the glycaemic index of the whole loaf.
  • Kefir instead of water or plain milk: live Lactobacillus cultures contribute mild fermentation that lowers the pH of the batter and begins partial starch modification, reducing post-meal glucose. Also adds gut-healing probiotic strains (Celiac).
  • Feta (½ cup): provides calcium that binds any trace dietary oxalate from olives and herbs in the gut before absorption — the kidney stone prevention strategy built directly into the recipe. Also delivers complete protein and B12 (MTHFR methylation support).
  • Garlic (3 cloves, crushed + 5 min rest): allicin activates AMPK (fatty liver / DP) and induces glutathione synthesis (MTHFR). The 5-minute pre-rest is not optional — heat immediately deactivates alliinase before allicin can form.
  • Kalamata olives: additional EVOO-equivalent polyphenols and MUFA; low oxalate; add savouriness without any sugar contribution.
  • Psyllium husk: soluble fibre that forms a gel matrix in the batter, slowing gastric emptying and glucose release — the GF bread equivalent of the gluten structure that does this in wheat bread. Also feeds beneficial gut bacteria (Celiac / microbiome).
  • Black pepper (on garlic and throughout): piperine in black pepper enhances bioavailability of polyphenols from EVOO and herbs — pair with turmeric in cooking for the 2000% curcumin absorption boost.
