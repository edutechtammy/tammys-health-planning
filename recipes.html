Normally, a strict, high-saturated-fat ketogenic diet is used to generate ketones. However, because you carry the APOE4 and FTO genes, high amounts of saturated fats (like heavy butter, coconut oil, or fatty red meats) can cause your blood lipids and liver fat to stall metabolically.

Instead, the clinical consensus is a "Clean" or Mediterranean Monounsaturated Ketosis. This leans heavily on Extra virgin olive oil (EVOO), walnuts, hemp seeds and other unsaturated fat food sources.

in a Mediterranean MIND diet, carbs are not cut back indiscriminately like you find in a KETO diet. Instead, the focus is on slow-burning, fiber-rich whole food carbohydrates. The goal is not ketosis. The goal is to protect the vascular lining and stabilize insulin response to prevent cognitive decline (APOE4). My specific adaptation is to move away from grains toward seeds. This is because my CGM data proves that I am highly reactive glycemically to all grains. Over time, all grain-based recipes will be replaced.

Carbs will come in the form of leafy greens (high in natural folate), cruciferous vegetables, berries (especially those that are high in polyphenols/antioxidants), nuts, seeds, and legumes. Fiber will be incorporated via the fiber naturally in these foods and with using natural binders such a psyllium husk powder and ground seeds (chia and flax - prebiotic fiber) which protect the gut microbiome.
