Perfect Hard Boiled Eggs — No Grey Ring
⏱ 2 min prep 🔥 10 min simmer 🧊 5 min ice bath
APOE4 Celiac / GF DP / Blood Sugar Fatty Liver MTHFR Kidney Stone

Cold eggs into cold water, bring to boil, 10 minutes at a low simmer, immediate ice bath. The grey-green ring is iron sulphide from overcooked H₂S — the 10-minute rule and ice bath prevent it entirely. The egg yolk is one of the most concentrated dietary sources of choline: directly neuroprotective for APOE4, and choline deficiency is a direct driver of NAFLD for fatty liver. Choline also converts to betaine which supports the methylation cycle via the BHMT bypass — relevant for MTHFR when the primary enzyme is impaired. Zero GI, zero oxalate. APOE4 frequency note: 4–5 eggs/week not daily.
